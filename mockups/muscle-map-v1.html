Fibrs muscle map: mockup v3

What's new: a detailed body with visible muscle fibres and tendons, a female body, full programmes, and a Profile page.

  1. Look closely at the body. The thin lines show which way each muscle's fibres run. Pale shapes are tendons, where muscle joins bone.
  2. Tap a muscle to zoom in and see its parts. Then tap any exercise card and the muscles it trains light up.
  3. Switch between Male and Female under the Front/Back switch.
  4. Search football and tap Add whole programme. Then open My plan.
  5. Open Profile to see your weekly muscle map and settings, including Light and Dark mode.
Sample content only. Every recommendation names its study, but a qualified coach or physiotherapist should review it all before launch. The drawing is an illustration, not a medical reference.
